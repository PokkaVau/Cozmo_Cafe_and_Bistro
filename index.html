Our Story

European Charm In The Heart of Basundhara

Cozmo Cafe & Bistro is a premier European and French-inspired culinary destination situated at Rupayan Shopping Square, Basundhara R/A.

Our space is crafted to feel like a portal into medieval British aristocracy fused with Parisian cafe culture. Featuring a signature indoor fireplace, magnificent life-sized thematic paintings, and ambient low-light jazz vibes, every corner is curated to be your cozy sanctuary and Dhaka's most Instagram-worthy retreat.
