# Cozmo Cafe & Bistro - Comprehensive Guide

**Cozmo Cafe & Bistro** is a premium European and French-inspired dining destination situated in the heart of Basundhara R/A, Dhaka. Known for its cozy, vibrant atmosphere reminiscent of medieval British aristocracy and Parisian elegance, it features life-sized thematic paintings, warm lighting, and a modern, highly Instagram-worthy interior.

## ✨ Ambiance & Amenities
* **The Vibe:** Casual, trendy, upscale, and romantic with a signature indoor fireplace setup and life-sized thematic paintings.
* **Events & Entertainment:** Regular live music sessions and big-screen events for major sports matches.
* **Special Facilities:** Private dining rooms available for events or meetings, along with designated smoking zones.
* **Accessibility:** Features a wheelchair-accessible entrance, seating, and restrooms.
* **Dietary Options:** Accommodates varied preferences with full-meal vegetarian and vegan options.
